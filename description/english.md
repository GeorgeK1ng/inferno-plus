# Inferno Plus

Inferno Plus makes Inferno more competitive, varied, and enjoyable while preserving the character of the original faction. It strengthens overlooked units, gives several heroes more useful starting setups and specialties, improves the role of Fire Magic, and adjusts a few town services.

## Creature changes

* Hell Hounds and Cerberi gain **Hunger**: if their first attack kills nothing, they attack again.
* Familiars strengthen direct-damage spells by 1 point per Familiar.
* Birthing Pools increase weekly Imp and Familiar growth by 12. Their greater benefit is balanced by a cost of 500 gold, 1 mercury, and 1 sulfur, as well as an Order of Fire prerequisite.

## Spell changes

* Dimension Door belongs to Fire Magic.
* Fireball is now level 2, Inferno and Slayer are level 3, Blind is level 4, and Misfortune is level 1.
* Fireball and Misfortune have lower casting costs.

## Hero changes

* Heretics begin with 2 Knowledge and Basic Fire Magic instead of Wisdom, allowing them to use their starting spells immediately.
* Octavia and Rashka begin with spell books and know View Earth and Curse, respectively.
* Zydar begins with Magic Arrow, Ayden with Curse, and Axsis with Stone Skin.
* Pyre begins with a Cannon and specializes in it, similarly to Jeremy.
* Olema specializes in Misfortune, casts it at Expert level, and reduces Luck by 4 instead of 2.
* Fiona allows her Hell Hounds and Cerberi to make one additional Hunger attack for every three hero levels.

## Town changes

* The Inferno Blacksmith sells Ballistas, which better suit a faction with only one natural ranged unit.
* The Dungeon Blacksmith sells Ammo Carts.

Inferno Plus is an experimental balance mod. Some values and specialties may change in response to playtesting and community feedback. Feedback is welcome on the VCMI Discord; contact **nineGardens**.
